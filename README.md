# Welcome to the sky time 

Imagine you are in a living in a post apocalyptic world where outside is a barren wasteland, ash clouds cover the sky, and darkness is everywhere. This programme simulates the wonderous palette of sky over 24 hours in real time. 

Check back at different times of the day. Switch off your lights, close the curtains, and load the webpage to check the time.
